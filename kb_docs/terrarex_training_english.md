## Section 2 — System Types

### Quick comparison

| System | How it works | During a power cut | Subsidy | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| On-Grid | Solar + grid, no battery | Shuts off | Yes | Areas with reliable supply |
| Hybrid | Solar + grid + battery | Keeps running | Yes | Areas with 3–4+ hours of cuts |
| Off-Grid | Solar + battery, no grid | Keeps running | No | Remote locations |

**Rule:** Wherever daily cuts exceed 3–4 hours, Hybrid is the right choice.

### Q: What system types do you offer? / Difference between On-Grid and Hybrid?
A:
1. *On-Grid* — Connected to the grid, no battery. Solar runs the home by day, surplus exports to the grid (Net Metering). Shuts off during a power cut. Cheapest, fastest payback. Best where the grid is stable.
2. *Hybrid* — Solar + battery backup. Keeps running during cuts; surplus stored in the battery. Best where daily cuts exceed 3–4 hours.

Quick rule: cuts under 2 hours → On-Grid. 3–4+ hours → Hybrid.
How many hours of cuts do you usually see?

### Q: What is Net Metering?
A:
Net Metering lets your electricity meter run both ways.
- During the day, solar makes power
- Your home consumes first
- Surplus flows to the grid (credits accumulate)
- At month-end you're billed only on the *net* units

Example: generated 500 units, used 300 → 200 units credit → bill ≈ ₹0.
Terra Rex files the net-meter application for you, free.

## Section 5 — DISCOM Rules & System Sizing

**Key rule:** Your plant size cannot exceed your Sanctioned Load. No exceptions.

Examples:
- Sanctioned Load 3 kW → max plant 3 kW
- Sanctioned Load 5 kW → max plant 5 kW
- Sanctioned Load 10 kW → max plant 10 kW

**Where to find Sanctioned Load?** Latest electricity bill — look for "Sanctioned Load" or "Connected Load".

Want a larger system? Apply to the DISCOM to increase your load — Terra Rex helps with that too.
